Privacy Policy — The Night Apothecary

Effective date: November 3, 2026

The Night Apothecary ("the Game") is published by AugustInnovations LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game handles.

Information we collect

The Game collects no information and shares no information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no third-party tracking, and it does not sell or share any data.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (the current night, reputation, stock, story flags, the endings you have found and settings) only on your device, in the app's local storage, so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data removes it.

Purchases

The Game has no in-app purchases. Everything in it is included in the app.

Network access

The whole Game is bundled inside the app. It makes no network requests and plays fully offline.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
